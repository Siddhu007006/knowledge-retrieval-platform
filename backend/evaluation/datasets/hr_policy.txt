EMPLOYEE LEAVE AND ONBOARDING POLICY

Annual leave entitlement
Full-time employees receive 18 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave is credited monthly at 1.5 days per completed month. Leave requests should be submitted at least five working days before the planned start date and require manager approval.

Sick leave
Employees receive 10 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. For an absence lasting more than two consecutive working days, the employee must provide a medical certificate to Human Resources. Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible on the first day of illness.

Parental leave
Eligible primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Eligible secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Employees should contact Human Resources at least 30 days before the planned leave date where practicable.

New employee onboarding
New employees must complete identity verification, tax and payroll forms, security awareness training, and the employee handbook acknowledgement during their first five working days. Their manager is responsible for assigning a role-specific onboarding buddy and arranging a first-week check-in.
